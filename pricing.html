06 — Questions

Asked before every project

Do I have to buy a domain and hosting myself?

No. We buy the domain, set up hosting and SSL, and keep everything renewed while you are on a Care Plan. The contract states plainly that the domain belongs to you and can be transferred to your own account at any time, free of charge. We do not hold anything hostage.

Why is the first month not free?

Because free teaches everyone that support is worth nothing, and months one to three are exactly when the work is heaviest. Instead we include three full months inside the project price and show you what they are worth. You get more, and it is honest.

What is a revision round?

One consolidated list of feedback during the review stage. A revision improves something we already agreed on. Something new that was never discussed is a scope change and is quoted separately. Extra rounds are $150.

What happens if I am slow sending content?

Your project pauses after 14 days and your slot is released to another client. Restarting costs $250. This is not a penalty, it is the only way a small studio can keep its promises to everyone else.

Who receives the money from online sales?

You do, directly. The Stripe account is always in your name and payouts go to your bank. We never touch our clients’ revenue. Stripe’s processing fee of 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction, sales tax, and chargebacks are yours to manage — we set the system up so it is straightforward.

Can I start small and grow later?

Yes, and most shops should. Start with Foundation and payment links, then upgrade to a full store for $1,600 when your orders justify it. We would rather build you the right thing twice than the wrong thing once.

Do you use AI to build these sites?

We use the best tools available to work faster and at higher quality. You are paying for the design, the decisions and the result — not for hours spent typing.

Will these prices last?

No. Launch pricing applies to our first five clients. After that we move to standard rates, which are shown crossed out beside every price on this page.
