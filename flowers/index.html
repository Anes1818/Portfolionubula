A few things you might be wondering

Room for
questions.

✻
Is this a full website for my shop?+

The $29 plan covers one configured bouquet builder on a Nebula-hosted link. You can share it from your existing website or social profiles. A complete website or custom domain is separate work with a separate quote.

Can I use my own flowers and prices?+

Your prices are configured for the existing flower artwork library, in one currency. We’ll check that the library fits your bouquets before setup. New flower photography, cutouts, species and custom artwork need a separate quote.

Does it take payments or confirm orders?+

It prepares a visual bouquet request for WhatsApp. The customer still presses Send, and you personally confirm availability, feasibility, delivery and the final price. There is no checkout, stock reservation or automatic order confirmation.

What do you need from me to get started?+

Your logo, shop name, contact details, currency and prices for the included flowers. We configure your version, include one setup review, and put your shop link live. Payment starts after your version is live.

What happens if I cancel?+

You can cancel anytime. Your builder stays available until the end of the paid period, with no further renewal. The hosted link is included while subscribed.
