  original geometry and all four original berry heads.
- The pricing page, enquiry values, two landing-page budget hints and new local

## Exact local basic-tier scope

**$29/month, one shop, one Nebula-hosted HTTPS link, no custom domain.** Initial
configuration from supplied logo/name/contact details, one currency and existing
catalog prices; existing artwork, Classic/Dome/Heart, EN/ES, PNG/design exports
and WhatsApp request/sheet. One setup review round; hosting and included-builder
maintenance; one monthly batch of up to **10 price/text/contact edits** and up to
**20 minutes asynchronous setup/use help**, without rollover. No basic setup fee.
Pay only after the shop’s version is live. Cancel anytime; service ends after
the paid period with no further renewal.

Separate quote: domain registration/connection/renewal (price unapproved), new
photography/cutouts/custom artwork, additional catalog species/layouts/pages or
languages, integrations, extra edits/review rounds/support and urgent work.
See [current marketing context](marketing/product-marketing.md),
[pricing page](build-your-plan.html) and [three Instagram concepts](marketing/pinned-post-carousel.md).

## What remains unproven

- No real-phone WhatsApp app launch/delivery or second-device receipt was tested.
  The saved drafts are not delivered messages. Localhost links are local only.
- A real florist must judge recognition, recipe/food suitability, availability,
  assembly and final prices. The photos’ commercial rights need owner confirmation.
- There is no payment, reservation, database or shared dashboard. Browser saves
  are local. Request links contain contact data; recipients should not forward them.
  Sheets use current shop prices, which can differ from an older draft/screenshot.
- No full accessibility certification, iOS Safari certification or guarantee for
  every device. Test with the intended shop’s phone before customer launch.
- Zero paying customers and no outreach since September 25 remain the business
  reality. Visual quality does not establish willingness to pay. Validate price
  acceptance, library fit, usefulness of received requests, hosting costs and the
  actual setup/support workload before broad rollout.
