# Sunflower Classic study — 18 October 2026

## Customization correction and optional gift

The sunflower finish now uses a stable 560px-wide wrap ending at y=810 and a fixed preview transform. Previously the wrap center/size and automatic camera fit depended on the moving flower bounds, making the entire composition appear to shift during a single-flower drag. The enlarged rear paper now extends above the original seven-flower canopy. Flower anchors remain individually editable; adding/replacing flowers does not rearrange the others. This is a bounded workspace, not an infinitely auto-growing paper; unusually large compositions need a separate layout review.

`gift-options.js` adds an optional chocolate gift box in the finishing panel for Classic, Dome and Heart. One box is a separate addition, never seven/eight artificial flower stems. It defaults off, can be removed, and persists in links, saved drafts, exported artwork, request summaries and florist sheets. The $15 rate is a provisional demo price requiring owner approval. Source `IMG_20261018_191316_770.png` is preserved under `assets/gifts/masters`, with lossless WebP runtime. Its outer alpha is real, but visible checkerboard pixels inside the clear lid are baked into the supplied artwork; the original has not been retouched. A cleaner source is still needed for final artwork quality.

`sunflower-customization.cjs` exercises actual mouse dragging and tap-to-replace, asserting unchanged anchors for every other flower and unchanged paper/camera. It checks adding a lily, optional gift UI/removal and the $15 delta in all three modes, fresh florist sheets, link reconstruction, exports and mobile widths. All 63 client regression checks pass.
