# Three Instagram concepts — LOCAL DRAFTS, not published

Use current screen recordings only. Keep “Demo · sample prices” readable. Do not
present the demo as a paying florist, claim guaranteed savings/ROI, or use Elena’s
photo, name or implied endorsement. No DMs or account changes are part of this work.

## 1. The conversation — 4-frame carousel

**Audience problem to test:** explaining a custom bouquet and estimating it from
an incomplete chat description.

1. “When someone asks ‘How much for a bouquet like this?’ — what do you still need to know?”
2. “Which pieces? How many? What wrapping? What date?” Use clearly labeled sample
   text, not an invented screenshot of a real customer conversation.
3. Show Classic with a familiar flower arrangement: “Let them show you a starting idea.”
4. Show the estimate and Order it: “Their choices → a WhatsApp request → your confirmation.”

**Caption:** “Custom bouquet conversations start with details. Nebula lets a customer
choose pieces, see an estimate from the shop’s configured prices, and prepare a
WhatsApp request. You still confirm availability, substitutions and the final price.
This is a demo with sample prices. Which detail do customers most often leave out?”

**CTA:** Comment with the missing detail. **Learn:** actual florist problems and vocabulary.

## 2. The build — 20-second Reel

0–3s: expanded Classic rose bouquet. Overlay: “Start with an idea.”
3–7s: choose a flower from the catalog and change one part of the bouquet.
7–12s: adjust the mix or quantity; keep the sample estimate visible.
12–16s: show wrapping and a small edit; use a real continuous capture, no fake taps.
16–20s: tap Order it. Overlay: “A request. Your shop confirms the final price.”

**Voiceover:** “Your customer can try a bouquet before you make it. They choose
the pieces and see an indicative estimate. Then they prepare a WhatsApp request
for your shop to review.”

**Caption:** “A customer can show you a bouquet idea, see an indicative estimate
and send the choices for your review. The demo uses sample prices; your shop's
version uses settings you approve. You still confirm availability and the final
price. Would the current flower library fit your shop?”

**CTA:** Try the demo link. **Learn:** whether a florist understands and values
the full bouquet-to-request workflow.

## 3. The handoff and offer — 25-second Reel

0–4s: Order it recap. “What does the florist actually receive?”
4–10s: show synthetic contact data, requested date and pickup preference.
10–16s: show the intercepted/local WhatsApp draft beside the bouquet sheet: item
quantities, finishes, estimate, contact details and sheet link. Label “Draft preview”.
Never imply a message was sent when only a draft exists. Use team-owned numbers
for a later real-device capture, with separate sending authorization.
16–21s: “You confirm stock and final price. No automatic payment.”
21–25s: “$29/month · no custom domain · pay after live · cancel anytime.”

**Caption:** “A visual request lands in the conversation you already use—after the
customer presses Send in WhatsApp. The basic tier is one Nebula-hosted builder,
setup from the existing artwork library, one setup review, one monthly batch of up
to 10 price/text/contact edits, and up to 20 minutes of asynchronous help monthly.
No rollover. New artwork, extra support and custom domains are quoted separately.
There is no checkout or shared order dashboard. See the full scope on our pricing page.”

**CTA:** Ask for the basic-tier scope. **Learn:** whether a shop accepts the actual
price and limits, and what blocks purchase. No revenue/customer claims.
