## Current commercial question

Can an independent shop that already takes custom bouquet orders through Instagram/WhatsApp use the existing design library and agree to one branded, price-configured link for $29/month? The last confirmed status in the available brief is zero paying florist shops. Ask for explicit scope and price acceptance, then verify a real shop setup and its WhatsApp handoff.

## Next 14-day test

1. Make the florist-facing page the proposed Instagram bio destination. Keep the live builder one tap away on that page. Record the date of any bio change so before/after numbers are not conflated.
2. Reply to FLOWER comments with the promised demo link and one qualifying question: “Do you take custom bouquet orders for your flower shop?” Do not ask permission to send a link they already requested. Thank non-shop users and collect optional product feedback; do not count them as sales leads.
3. For shop owners who answer, ask how they currently receive custom orders. Then show the bounded $29/month scope, no basic setup fee, payment after go-live, and the fact that the florist confirms availability and final quote. Ask directly whether they would use this for their shop. Avoid promising their unique bouquets are included as new artwork.
4. Keep a simple log by account: shop owner verified, custom-order workflow, demo tried, $29 scope discussed, objection, explicit yes/no, next action. One useful follow-up after several business days is enough when someone goes quiet; stop on a clear no.
5. Work through up to 20 suitable independent florists over roughly 14 days, as the October 9 brief proposes. Test the actual customer-to-WhatsApp route on a phone before configuring the first shop. Configure one shop only after written acceptance of the bounded scope and approved prices.

## Ad-spend decision boundary

Do not infer campaign profitability or recommend scaling, killing or restructuring the live ad from these screenshots. Spend, objective, targeting, cost per qualified shop conversation, and any actual price acceptances are not shown. Hold off on increasing budget until those are logged. Review the account using qualified florist conversations and explicit $29 decisions, rather than comments and link taps alone.

This note records an experiment, not a sales forecast. No profile, campaign, site, message or price was changed by creating it.
