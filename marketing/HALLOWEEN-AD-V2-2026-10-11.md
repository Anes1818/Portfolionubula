# Halloween ad v2 — product in the first frame

Prepared October 11, 2026 after the founder rejected the static chat-led opening as dated. This replaces the v1 creative direction. It does not delete or change existing Canva designs.

## What changed

Start with the actual editable spooky bouquet and a visible touch, not a title card or an empty chat screen. A short example customer question overlays the action, and the demonstration answers it immediately. The product occupies the screen from frame zero. Chat is a small contextual layer, not the format of the whole ad.

Audience: independent florist owners. English creative; Arabic production guidance. Length: 20 seconds as an initial production target, adjustable for readable footage. One action: comment FLOWER for the demo. Main benefit: a visual starting point and estimate for the custom-request conversation, with the florist retaining final judgment.

## Grounding

The founder suggested customer questions about red flowers and spooky bouquets, and reports florist-owner comments on the old FLOWER ad. In the September export, the September 13 reel opened with how often florists typed prices into DMs and used FLOWER; it recorded 33 shares and 19 saves. Its exact body included wording that overstated a request as an order; do not repeat that wording. The archived video itself has not been reviewed. These are content/evidence inputs, not proof that this new creative will perform.

The product supports actual rose replacements, ghosts and the estimate/request flow. Use Forever My Boo's editable preview and real UI. Current media can be inspected at `builder/tests/halloween/forever-my-boo.png`; it is an actual renderer export with sample pricing, not a fulfilled customer bouquet.

## Primary script — action before explanation

Keep a small readable `FOR FLORISTS` identifier in the upper safe area through the opening. Small `Example request · builder demo` context accompanies the customer questions. Do not show fake customer names or a staged sale.

| Time | Exact text / voice | Actual shot |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Large question: **“More red?”**. Optional voice: **“A Halloween request starts like this.”** | The editable bouquet is visible from frame zero. A finger/cursor selects a black rose, then replaces it with red. Start at the action, not the gallery. |
| 2–5s | Small second question: **“Keep the ghosts?”**. Main text: **Let them try the idea.**. Voice: **“Your customer can try the design.”** | Make the next rose replacement; hold the changed bouquet with ghosts still visible. No typing sequence or staged reply. |
| 5–8s | Question: **“How much?”**. Main text: **An estimate from your prices.**. Voice: **“The estimate uses your configured prices.”** | Show actual item quantities and sample estimate. Do not invent an increase; replacements can have the same rate. |
| 8–12s | **Design + details → WhatsApp request**. Voice: **“They review the details and send you a WhatsApp request.”** | Review request, then prepare the actual WhatsApp draft and design link. The recording stops before sending. Small line: **Customer presses Send**. |
| 12–16s | **You confirm the arrangement + quote.**. Voice: **“You confirm availability, the arrangement and the final quote.”** | Return to the matching bouquet or linked sheet. No inventory check or confirmed-order badge is implied. |
| 16–20s | **Florists: comment FLOWER** / **for the demo link.** Small line: **Your shop link: $29/month**. Voice: **“Comment FLOWER for the demo.”** | Keep the final bouquet visible. Overlay one large, clear action rather than cutting to a long terms slide. Full commercial terms sit in the caption. |

Do not rush text just to achieve 20 seconds. If the request recording needs extra reading time, extend that segment. One clear action per piece. No additional follow/save/DM instruction.

## التسجيل المطلوب

ابدأ التسجيل داخل التصميم القابل للتعديل، بحيث تظهر الباقة فورًا. لا تسجّل مقدمة طويلة أو تنقّلًا بين الصفحات قبل التعديل.

1. لقطة قريبة وواضحة: تغيير وردة سوداء إلى حمراء، ثم ثلاث وردات أخرى. الأشباح تبقى ظاهرة. اترك وقفة قصيرة قبل وبعد كل تعديل.
2. لقطة التقدير وقائمة العناصر. أظهر الرقم الحقيقي دون تغييره للمونتاج.
3. لقطة مراجعة الطلب ومسودة واتساب ببيانات تجريبية؛ توقف قبل الإرسال.
4. لقطة أخيرة للباقة المعدلة، ثابتة أربع ثوانٍ.

يمكن تسجيلها في جلسة واحدة، أو كأربع لقطات قصيرة. لا حاجة إلى وجهك أو ممثل أو مؤثرات مسبقة. أرفق الملفات الأصلية. سنقصّ الانتظار، ونضع الأسئلة فوق الفعل المناسب، ونحافظ على وضوح التعديل.
