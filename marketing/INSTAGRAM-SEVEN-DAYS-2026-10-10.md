## One-person recording checklist

### Prepare once

- [ ] Use the current `builder-upgrade` release for recording. The active `main` files are older. Use its existing delivery/demo copy or intentionally check out the correct release in your recording workspace before filming. Do not splice incompatible versions together.
- [ ] Check the destination a viewer will receive matches the demonstrated release and current $29 offer. This task has not deployed or verified a public version. Keep the package in production until the demo route is ready.
- [ ] Use a phone screen recording in portrait; export Reels/Stories at 1080 × 1920. Turn off notifications. No second camera or florist shop needed.
- [ ] Open Always You → Editable design preview → Customize. Avoid the generated Style image as a functional demonstration.
- [ ] Use a made-up demo name and non-personal test contact details. Avoid showing private chats, notifications or full encoded links containing personal details.
- [ ] Test the real-phone WhatsApp draft handoff using a destination you control. Inspect the draft and matching sheet. Stop before sending. No sending or account changes are authorized by this package.

### Capture one continuous raw take, then a short pickup

- [ ] Starting bouquet; two compatible rose replacements; paper-tone change.
- [ ] One priced extra on/off, with the corresponding estimate change and itemized details.
- [ ] Review request; requested date and pickup preference; preparation of actual WhatsApp draft; readable design-link text.
- [ ] Matching bouquet sheet with design and quantities. Label it as a sample.
- [ ] Back in the editor, one alternative flower edit and revised estimate for Reel 2.
- [ ] Hold each finished state 3–5 seconds so the editor has clean footage to trim. Record raw taps at ordinary speed; cuts can shorten pauses without pretending the whole process takes 30 seconds.
- [ ] Capture stills for carousel slides from this same run. Existing test PNGs on `builder-upgrade` can guide framing; fresh captures keep the design and UI consistent across posts.
- [ ] Record both exact voiceovers in a quiet room. If a take runs long, give it a few more seconds rather than rushing. Add matching subtitles and keep the main overlay distinct from subtitles.

### Assemble and review

- [ ] Keep controls, estimates and text readable on a real phone. Crop around each action; do not shrink the full desktop screen into the frame.
- [ ] Keep overlays away from the top/bottom app controls. Use a solid or translucent backing where flowers make text hard to read. Check the opening hook without sound.
- [ ] Preserve “sample estimate” context. The $29/month service price must be visually separate from the bouquet estimate.
- [ ] Show customer choices, estimate, WhatsApp preparation and florist review accurately. No auto-payment, stock detection, reservation, approval workflow or order dashboard is implied.
- [ ] Keep generated collection illustrations out of these proof shots. If later used decoratively, label them “AI-generated style illustration”; never describe them as fulfilled orders or exact editable results.
- [ ] Do not present a demo shop as a paying client, a sample request as a sale, or a hypothetical question as a testimonial.
- [ ] One response action per piece: DM DEMO, the story poll, or Reply DEMO. No extra save/follow/share instruction.
- [ ] Copy is complete; replace no terms with retired $650/$49 prices, a free trial or unlimited support. Full scope remains available for an interested owner.
