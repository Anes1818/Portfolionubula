# Nebula Bouquet Builder — current context

Document version: 1.0 · 29 September 2026 · local release draft

This supersedes the historical $650 setup / $49 monthly offer, Catalog offer,
30-day trial, unlimited revision/support promises and ROI claims. Those are not
current sales terms. The old context and carousel mentioned in the resume were
not present in this checkout; these local drafts establish the current version.

## Product and audience

For independent florists who discuss custom bouquets with customers on WhatsApp.
The hypothesis: a visual starting point, itemized estimate and readable request
can make the conversation clearer. No measured time saving or sales increase is
claimed. The existing vanilla builder has Classic, Dome and Heart; EN/ES; PNG
sharing; local/exported design saves; a WhatsApp draft; and a linked bouquet sheet.
The florist personally confirms stock, feasibility and final price.

No automated payment, deposits, confirmed orders, stock reservation, backend,
database, accounts or shared dashboard. Current prices and destination are demo
settings. A generated draft is not a delivered message.

## Basic tier — $29/month, no custom domain

- One shop and one Nebula-hosted HTTPS builder link while subscribed.
- Existing layouts and artwork library; initial configuration of supplied logo,
  shop name, contact details, one currency and existing catalog prices.
- One setup review round for those settings before launch.
- Existing EN/ES, three bouquet modes, PNG/design exports and WhatsApp handoff.
- Maintenance of the included builder.
- One batch of up to 10 price/text/contact edits per month.
- Up to 20 minutes of asynchronous setup/use help per month. No rollover of edits
  or support time; no live/on-call support or guaranteed response/restoration SLA.
- No basic setup fee. Payment only after the shop’s version is live. Cancel
  anytime; service remains until the paid period ends, without further renewal.

Separate written quote: custom-domain registration/connection/renewal (no approved
price yet), new flower/wrap photography, cutouts, retouching, custom artwork,
additional species/layouts/pages/languages, integrations, extra edit batches,
extra review rounds, additional support and urgent work. Do not promise that
every requested integration is feasible or currently available.

This is the bounded local offer prepared for this release, not a validated service
margin. Check hosting cost, setup time and actual support load with the first shops.
Before real use, confirm library fit, asset rights, supported devices, prices,
WhatsApp destination, food/assembly suitability and cancellation details with the
shop. Do not promise unrestricted artwork ownership or an accessibility certification.

## Evidence and open business assumptions

Zero paying florist customers. No outreach since the 25 September Instagram export.
The current Instagram offer is $29/month, no domain, payment after live, cancel
anytime. A prettier demo does not validate willingness to pay.

Still to learn: whether florists recognize the fruit without explanation, whether
customers can finish the flow on real phones, whether requests are useful to the
shop, whether the current library fits its products, whether $29 is acceptable,
and whether delivery costs/support time fit the basic scope. Record objections,
trial usage and actual price acceptance; views and likes do not answer these.
