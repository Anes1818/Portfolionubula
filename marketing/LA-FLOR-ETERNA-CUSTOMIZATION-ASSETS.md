## Shared acceptance standard

One object per file. Actual PNG RGBA transparency, not a painted checkerboard. Keep all tips and 8–10% clear padding. Soft light from upper left; neutral white balance. No floor, cast shadow, lettering, watermark or artificial glow. Retain fine petal/leaf edges. Review at the size used in the bouquet as well as full resolution. WebP conversion and naming come after approval.

## 2. Matching upright yellow paper layers

The supplied yellow paper remains visibly synthetic. Generate the complete wrapper first; derive both layers from that same approved image. Do not independently generate two wrappers.

Prompt for the master:

> Photorealistic empty florist bouquet wrapper made from soft butter-yellow matte paper, held upright and viewed straight from the front, camera slightly above the flower opening. Broad open upper cup, gently asymmetric folded side wings, natural thin paper thickness, subtle creases and overlapping sheets. A narrow gathered neck in the lower quarter, with a short visible paper tail below it. The front lip is low enough to reveal the lower flower heads when flowers are inserted. No flowers, foliage, stems, ribbon, hands or vase. Full silhouette with every corner visible. Genuine transparent PNG background, soft upper-left light, no background pattern. Portrait canvas 1600 × 2000, 10% clear padding. Avoid a flat fan, balloon-like folds, glossy plastic and a closed paper cone.

Then use the approved master for two isolation edits:

> Preserve the exact canvas, camera, scale, position, color and folds of this reference. Isolate ONLY the [rear and side wrapper / front overlapping lip, gathered neck and lower paper tail] onto genuine transparency. Do not redraw, recenter, enlarge or crop it. The two extracted layers must align perfectly when overlaid on the original canvas.

Acceptance: front paper covers stems, leaves flower faces visible, and the bow knot can sit at the actual neck. Save an alignment preview before importing either layer.

## 4. White filler with a top-facing tip

The current stock-flower sprig is suitable as a short upright accent. For the most convincing overhead arrangement, obtain a distinct top-facing terminal cluster.

> A single small terminal cluster of fresh ivory stock flowers (Matthiola), seen from directly above its flower faces as it would appear between roses in a hand-tied bouquet. Three to five small uneven blossoms, a few discreet pale green buds, very short branching connections mostly hidden beneath the blossoms. Compact irregular triangular silhouette, not a long vertical flowering stem and not a large round focal flower. True photographic petals, soft upper-left lighting, genuine transparent PNG with all edges visible and 10% margin. Match the color and texture of the attached ivory stock reference. No paper, rose, bouquet or checkerboard.
