# One-shop pilot reel — proposal for founder review

Date: 16 October 2026. This is proposed copy and a bounded pilot offer. It does not change the published $29 plan, launch a trial, publish a post, or send any message.

## Decision

Select **one** independent flower shop that already handles custom bouquet requests and is willing to use the builder with real customers. Do not advertise three shop slots when setup capacity is one. The trial starts when that shop's link is live, not when it first DMs. Halloween is a timely example within a year-round builder; there are only about two weeks until October 31, so do not guarantee a pre-Halloween launch without confirming the shop's inputs and testing.

## Recommended pilot terms to approve before posting

- One Nebula-hosted shop builder link for 30 days from go-live, using the existing 10-design library, including the three Spooky Bouquets. The existing lightweight collection page is part of this link. A new catalog of the shop's own bouquet photos or new artwork is outside this pilot.
- Configure the owner's supplied shop name, logo, WhatsApp contact, one currency and approved prices for existing catalog items. One setup review round. Verify destination and customer-to-WhatsApp flow on phones before launch.
- No basic setup fee or card collected. The free access ends after 30 days unless the shop explicitly opts in to the current $29/month plan. No automatic charge. Paid plan cancellation remains as described in the current offer.
- Florist confirms stock, feasibility, final arrangement and final quote. Collection style images are inspiration, not proof of fulfilled customer orders or guarantees of exact final arrangements.
- Choose a shop whose current bouquets fit the existing library, can supply approved prices and branding, and is willing to share the link with customers during the month. Ask for honest feedback; testimonial or public case study use requires separate consent.
- Log setup hours, hosting/support effort, whether any real requests reach the florist, the owner's objections, and whether they explicitly choose $29/month after the trial. A free activation alone does not validate willingness to pay.

## DM qualification and success measure

Reply to SHOP DMs with one question: “Thanks for your interest 🌸 Do you currently take custom bouquet requests through Instagram or WhatsApp for your flower shop?” If yes, ask for the shop name and a short description of its usual custom bouquets. Explain that current designs are included and new artwork is a separate quote before accepting the pilot.

Measure qualified owner DMs, shops matching the library, accepted written scope, actual customer use, useful requests received, setup/support hours, and explicit paid continuation. Do not use total story votes, reel comments or free sign-ups as the success criterion.
