# Nebula: first paying florist plan — 30 September 2026

## Decision

The whole bouquet builder is ready for a **bounded paid pilot offer**, subject to
publishing the current release and checking one real-phone handoff. It is not yet
validated demand: no florist has paid, accepted the $29 scope, or received a real
customer request through it. Do not make strawberry artwork the sales story.

## Offer to test

For $29/month, give one florist a Nebula-hosted builder link configured with the
shop's name, logo, WhatsApp number, one currency, and approved prices for the
existing catalog. No basic setup fee. Payment begins after the shop's version is
live; cancellation can happen anytime. The shop confirms availability and final
price. The local pricing page defines one setup review, one monthly batch of up
to 10 price/text/contact edits, and up to 20 minutes of asynchronous help. These
limits are a proposed service boundary; confirm them before quoting a real shop.
New artwork, domain work, integrations, and a full website are separate quotes.

## Before directing traffic to the site

1. Publish the current `builder/`, `build-your-plan.html`, `flowers/`, and the new
   `flower/` redirect together. The public site still has the retired $650 + $49
   offer, an older builder, and a 404 at `/flower` as of this audit. Do not send
   prospects to that mismatch.
2. On a real phone, build a bouquet, open the WhatsApp draft using a team-owned
   number, press Send, open the linked sheet on a second phone, and confirm the
   estimate is visibly provisional. The browser tests intercepted the draft;
   they did not prove delivery on a physical device.
3. Check the $29 plan's actual monthly hosting and support cost, and approve the
   exact included edit/support limits before accepting a customer.

## First seven days after the public update

- Day 1: record a 15–20 second screen demo of a normal rose bouquet: choose,
  change, see estimate, open the request. Use the current release, not an old
  screenshot. Label demo prices as samples.
- Day 2: post one problem-led Instagram carousel: “A customer asks for a custom
  bouquet. What details do you still need before you can price it?” Show the
  choices the builder collects. Ask florists which detail is usually missing.
- Days 2–5: identify 20 independent florists who sell custom bouquets and use
  Instagram or WhatsApp with customers. Use LinkedIn Premium and Instagram for
  research; log fit, contact date, reply, objection, demo request, and explicit
  price acceptance in Notion Business. Do not treat views or follows as leads.
- Days 3–7: send up to five tailored, short outreach messages per day. Mention one
  product type the shop actually sells. Offer a 30-second demo and ask whether
  their current order conversation would benefit from the customer sending a
  visual choice and estimate. Send the link after interest, not as a first-line
  demand to inspect a website. Keep follow-up to one useful message.
- Day 7: review the counts: owners reached, replies, demo views, calls or voice
  notes, specific objections, shops agreeing to the $29 scope, and one pilot
  willing to provide shop settings. Change the offer based on objections before
  adding features or buying ads.

## Outreach opening to personalize

> Hi [name], I saw your [specific custom bouquet]. When customers describe a
> bouquet in DMs, do they usually send enough detail to price it? I made a small
> visual builder that lets them choose flowers and sends you their idea plus an
> estimate to review on WhatsApp. Would a 30-second demo be useful?

If they say yes, show a normal flower bouquet first. Then explain: “One shop link
is $29/month, no custom domain, pay after it is live, cancel anytime. Your
approved prices go in; you confirm the final order.” Ask what would stop them
from using it with one customer. Record their answer verbatim.

## Resource rule

Use Notion for the evidence log and LinkedIn Premium to find owners. Keep the
Lovable credits for a specific recurring blocker discovered in those talks;
there is no reason to rebuild the existing builder now. VPN and Headspace do not
change the product, price, or distribution plan. Hold paid Instagram ads until
the public offer is consistent and at least one florist has accepted the actual
price and workflow.
