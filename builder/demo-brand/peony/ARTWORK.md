# Peony & Pearl

Added 26 October 2026 to the Nebula demo. Eight apricot peonies, 22 ivory roses,
five editable eucalyptus sprigs and cream ruffle wrapping. Prices are sample
estimates. The peony is available in Dome and Heart; no Classic stem asset is
claimed. The new head uses a 1.4 relative size in the mixed-bloom layout so its
larger petals have room among the roses. Existing bouquet sizes stay unchanged.

`apricot-peony-v1.png` is an original transparent asset generated with the built-in
image generation tool. The original alpha is preserved. `peony-and-pearl.webp`
is rendered by `builder/tests/peony-bouquet.cjs` from the actual editable design,
not a separately generated bouquet photograph.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: individual flower sprite for a photorealistic interactive bouquet builder. Generate ONE beautiful fully open soft apricot / blush-peach peony blossom, viewed directly from above, flower face pointing exactly at the camera. A richly layered, rounded head of fine ruffled petals, warm ivory at some outer edges, natural subtle color variation, no visible yellow stamen center. Carefully photographed realistic fresh flower, soft diffuse neutral studio daylight, crisp natural petal detail. Composition: square image, single isolated blossom centered, circular overall silhouette, blossom fills 88% of canvas with generous transparent padding around all petals. Genuine transparent alpha background. NO stem, NO leaves, NO other flowers, NO bouquet, NO wrapping, NO text, NO watermark, NO cast shadow outside the flower. Keep the natural asymmetry of real petals; no hard outlined illustration or plastic texture.
