# For Love collection

Three editable year-round templates sit above the four Autumn templates:

| Template | Flowers | Separate details |
|---|---|---|
| Pink Promise | 44 pink roses | 12 baby’s breath sprigs, ivory wrap |
| Written in Roses | 44 red roses | One editable floral initial, black wrap |
| Always You | 30 red roses | 6 eucalyptus sprigs, 6 baby’s breath sprigs, ivory wrap |

All use the current organic Dome layout. Gallery, builder, order sheet and PNG
export use the same scene. Flowers remain editable one by one. These are
fixed-view interpretations of the supplied references, not reconstructions of
the taller hand-tied bouquets.

## New controls and accounting

- Baby’s breath: scattered between flowers or distributed around the round
  edge. The count stays explicit, with the existing per-sprig charge. Changing
  pattern leaves the flower recipe and price unchanged. Border sprigs
  redistribute when their count changes; scattered sprigs retain their prefix.
- Interior eucalyptus: 0–12 sprigs, independently counted and priced using
  `flowerPrices.eucalyptus`. Existing long photographed sprigs are positioned
  between blooms; no new artwork was generated or retouched.
- Floral initial: one A–Z letter, size 70–130%, vertical offset ±35% of the
  bouquet radius. The sample price is $12 for one composed ornament, configured
  through `extrasCents.floralInitial`. Its small texture repetitions are not
  billed as full filler stems. Florist confirmation still applies.
- New values are bounded and validated. Undo, local save, JSON, shared links,
  English/Spanish labels and the florist sheet preserve the details.
- Autumn templates retain their recipes. No 3D code or deployment was added.

## One requested asset

The current initial uses the existing airy baby’s breath image as a dense
repeated texture. Glyph masks select sprite centres; the sprite edges remain
natural. It is a functional preview, but a compact white tuft will better
match the floral initial in the reference.

Generate one asset and remove the background; save as
`babys-breath-dense.png` with real alpha:

> Photorealistic overhead product photograph of one small compact tuft of
> fresh white baby's breath (Gypsophila), approximately 20–30 tiny white
> florets tightly gathered into an irregular rounded cluster. White flowers
> dominate, minimal green visible, no long stems or leaves. Natural fluffy
> outline, sharp delicate petal detail, soft diffuse light from upper left.
> Entire tuft centred with generous empty margin, no cropping. Isolated on a
> uniform medium-gray background for clean background removal. No bouquet,
> wrapping, ribbon, letters, text, watermark, cast ground shadow or checkerboard.

The later lily/orchid mix and decorated heart need their own layout and asset
work. This stage does not claim to implement those two references.

## Validation

`tests/fall/romance.cjs` checks all new templates, pattern/count accounting,
initial controls and all 26 generated letter textures, undo, validation,
precise shared-scene and price round-trips, persistence, florist details,
320/390 px mobile operation, localization and offline PNG export. Images and
results are saved alongside the test. The existing template, detail and
asset/edge suites also pass.
