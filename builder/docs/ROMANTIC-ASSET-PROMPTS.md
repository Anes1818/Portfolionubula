## 2. eucalyptus-sparse-b.png

## 3. rose-red-cup-a.png

Photorealistic florist product photograph of a single fresh deep crimson red
rose head, medium-open and cup-shaped. Natural velvety petals with irregular
layering, slightly folded outer petals, a deep shadowed centre. Camera looking
down at approximately 35 degrees from directly overhead, showing both the
flower opening and the height of the front petals. Match the red roses in the
attached bouquet reference. Soft diffuse daylight from upper left, neutral
colour, crisp fine petal detail. Only the flower head, with minimal calyx hidden
under the petals, no long stem or leaves. Centre the entire bloom on pure white
with margin all around. No perfectly geometric spiral, glossy plastic texture,
vase, text, background shadow or checkerboard.

## 4. rose-red-cup-b.png

Use the same prompt and reference as asset 3. Create a different rose, more open,
with a broader irregular outline and a few softly curled outer petals. View it
from 20 degrees off overhead. Keep the same crimson colour and lighting. Do not
duplicate or rotate the first flower. Keep the entire head inside the frame.
