## Priority 1: a left-facing lily

Attach the original real bouquet photograph as a flower-style reference.

> Create one isolated photographic cutout of a fresh pink Oriental lily, viewed at a three-quarter angle with its open cup facing slightly left. Match the real bouquet reference: deep raspberry-pink petal centers, broad clean white edges, sparse natural speckles, long visible stamens and irregular petals curling backwards. Show genuine foreshortening: the near petals spread forward and the far petals are partially hidden. The entire flower must be visible with 10 percent clear margin. Only a very short hidden neck at the base; no leaves, no long stem, no other flowers, no paper, no decorations or text. Soft neutral daylight from the upper left, believable botanical texture, no cast shadow outside the flower. Transparent background, high-resolution PNG, at least 1200 pixels on the long side. Make a real flower photograph, avoiding a perfectly symmetrical star shape or plastic-looking petals.

## Priority 2: a different right-facing lily

> Create one isolated photographic cutout of a different fresh pink Oriental lily, with its open cup turned slightly right and upwards. Deep raspberry-pink petal centers, broad white edges, sparse speckles, slender stamens, strongly curved petals with some natural unevenness. Use an oblique view with a clear cup and one side partly foreshortened. It should belong to the same variety and lighting setup as the reference but have a different petal silhouette. No wrapper, other flowers, decoration or text. Soft neutral daylight from the upper left, realistic texture and natural shadow inside the petals, no external cast shadow. Transparent high-resolution PNG, at least 1200 pixels on the long side. Avoid flat overhead symmetry and avoid simply mirroring another lily.
