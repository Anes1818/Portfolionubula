# Natural strawberry Dome candidate — 30 September 2026

Local visual review only. Three generated transparent assets in this directory
are bundled as `assets/heads/strawberry-natural-{1,2,3}.webp`. Their source
prompts and provenance are recorded in `generation.json`. They are generated
fruit images, not photographs of a florist's stock.

The current Dome chooses these three images by slot, with small rotations and
back-to-front overlap. The white paper collar, slot positions, berry dimensions,
pricing, and saved-link format were retained. Heart artwork and old Classic
strawberry designs still work. No rosemary was added to Dome.

The [current gallery](../../tests/visual-revision-2026-09-29/index.html) contains
fresh 390 × 844 normal phone captures and 1080 × 1080 unlabeled art exports for
30, 44, and 62 berries. The larger exports have a recognizable strawberry shape
and fine seeds. At normal phone size the fruit is small and the repeated upright
poses can read as red cones; the 62-piece arrangement is most crowded. The
hidden calyxes and synthetic uniformity limit realism. Visual recognition has
not been independently tested with shop owners.

The latest code passed all 100 local checks: 57 release checks, 20 overhead
behavior checks, and 23 focused comparison checks, with zero failures. These
cover geometry and price invariants, editing and Undo, legacy links, 1080px
export, offline loading, and phone layout. Result JSON files sit beside the
scripts in `tests/release-2026-09-29` and `tests/visual-revision-2026-09-29`.
No deployment or publishing was performed.
