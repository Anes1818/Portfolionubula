## Budget and boundaries

No paid provider plan or auto-upgrade was enabled. Free quotas are finite and shared; there is no unlimited-user promise. Per-shop active media is bounded to 18 images and retained bytes to 12 MiB. Pricing validation runs inside the Durable Object to avoid rebuilding all bouquet geometry on Worker Free requests. Static files bypass Worker execution, and the registry uses indexed pagination.
