The exact purple stem and dark flower are tentative identifications. |

Existing pink, ivory, cream and peach roses, sunflowers, and rust chrysanthemums cover the matching stems without duplicating them. The new white lily bud comes from the separate request. Both lily buds can be placed together and share the ten-bud capacity. They are billed separately and keep saved positions by variety.

Fifteen fresh heads can be moved freely in Classic or painted into Dome and Heart slots. Gerberas, lime hydrangeas and the red daisy mum sit above the surrounding blooms. Black and peach callas keep a slight upright lean and use the silhouettes of nearby petals to cover the bottom of the cup. Chamomile, hypericum and bupleurum tuck into the gaps. Purple stock and golden button mums favour gaps near the edge. The five sprigs and both lily buds use the filler layer in Classic, Dome and Heart, so they preserve flower slots. A sprig already saved in a flower slot stays there. The white foam rose appears in the Satin and eternal library. Florists set their own prices in the catalogue.

Shape references for the calla and ranunculus identifications are the [RHS calla guide](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/zantedeschia) and [RHS ranunculus entry](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/525290/ranunculus-asiaticus-vortex-white/details). The names describe visible forms and colours from the photographs.

The [Nebula demo gallery](https://app.nebulastudio.site/demo/#reference-title) has six editable recipes based on the five references. Each preview is rendered by the builder from the same recipe that opens when you click it.

The counts are builder recipes inspired by the references. They do not claim the florists’ original stem counts. Golden Autumn uses the existing pumpkin decoration; the photos' acorns and bare twigs are omitted. Closed lily buds remain optional in fresh designs. They, like the sprigs, add to the price without changing the number of flower slots.

## Image generation prompts
