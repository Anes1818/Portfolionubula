Before you decide

Does it take payment or confirm orders?

No. It prepares a WhatsApp draft. There is no automated payment, deposit, stock reservation, database or shared dashboard. You arrange payment and confirm the order with your customer.

Can I use my own flower and wrapping photos?

The $29 tier uses the existing artwork library. Preparing new assets, photographing products and matching custom wraps require a separate quote.

Can I change prices myself?

There is no shop dashboard. Basic service includes one monthly batch of up to 10 price/text/contact edits. Extra changes are quoted before work starts.

Where are designs and customer details stored?

Designs save in the customer’s browser or an exported file. A request link contains the submitted contact details, so it should only be shared with the intended florist. There is no shared order database.

How do I decide if it fits my shop?

Try the live builder first. Check that its current flower library represents what you sell, then review the estimate and WhatsApp request flow before asking for your shop’s setup.
