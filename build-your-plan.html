Questions you might have

I already have Instagram. Why do I need this?

This does not replace Instagram — it feeds it. You put one link in your bio, and instead of answering twenty messages about sizes and prices, the customer arrives having already built her bouquet and accepted the price.

Where do the orders actually go?

Straight to your WhatsApp, as a message that is already written out — which bouquet, what size, the card note and the total. You reply to confirm, exactly like you do now. Nothing new to learn, no dashboard to check.

You do not touch anything. You send me a photo or a message on WhatsApp and I make the change for you. That is what the monthly fee is for.
