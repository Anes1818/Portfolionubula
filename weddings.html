When do my flowers arrive?
We build your bouquet the day before and deliver it styled on your wedding morning — so every bloom opens at its peak in your photos, not three days early in a shipping box.
Will I see it before the day?
Yes — you get a photo of your actual bouquet on WhatsApp the day before, and one round of changes is always free.
How do they stay fresh all day?
Every stem travels in water until the last minute, and we hand you a simple card: cool room, out of direct sun, a light mist — your flowers will outlast the dance floor.
What about allergies?
Tell us — we design low-pollen bouquets with roses, hydrangea and eucalyptus that are gentle on noses and still completely real.
