Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

How does pickup work & notice required?

All orders are for pickup in Gresham & Rockwood, Oregon. Fresh rose ramos require 3–4 days advance notice. Preserved eternal roses require 2 weeks notice.

How do I place and confirm my order?

Design your ramo right here in the online studio or pick from the catalog. Tapping Order sends your recipe directly via Instagram DM or web order so Julia can confirm date and time.

Can I add crowns, sashes & butterflies?

Absolutely! We offer sparkling tiara crowns ($4, $10, $15), custom printed message sashes ($5), 3D gold butterflies, and pearl mesh to make your ramo unforgettable.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Zelle and Cash App. Once your bouquet details and pickup slot are locked in, payment details are provided directly.
