Why the wordmark is live text, not part of the SVG

Baking “Petal Press” into the logo file would freeze it as decoration: unselectable, invisible to search engines, impossible to translate, and blurry when scaled. The mark is a drawing; the name is text in Instrument Serif. That keeps the header accessible and lets the Spanish build swap the tagline without touching a single asset.

05

The button, rebuilt

Same feel as the reference you sent. Four real defects removed. Hover it — the frame wobbles and the highlighter sweeps; the letters never move.

What changed

  • .highlight was position:absolute with no positioned ancestor, so it anchored to the page instead of the button. The wrapper now sets position:relative.
  • numOctaves="8" means eight layers of per-pixel Perlin noise, recomputed over the whole element. Capped at 3 — visually identical, a fraction of the cost.
  • animation:idle 1s infinite forced the browser to re-rasterise that filter forever, on every button, even untouched. The filter now runs on :hover and :focus-visible only, and never on touch devices where it cannot fire at all.
  • The filter sat on the text, smearing the glyphs. It now sits on a frame layer behind the label, so the wobble is intact and the words stay sharp and readable.
  • Added: prefers-reduced-motion disables the whole thing, focus rings are visible, and every target clears 44 px.
06

The ink pack

Fourteen doodles, 12.9 KB for the whole sheet, all in currentColor so a season can recolour them with one CSS variable. They are generated by a seeded script from three primitives — petal, wobbly circle, bowed stem — which is why they share one hand, and why a completely new pack takes about a second to produce.
