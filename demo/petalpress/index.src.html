Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

How does same-day delivery work?

Place your order before 2:00 PM EST, and our couriers will deliver it directly to your recipient's door today across Alexandria, Arlington, Fairfax, Tysons, Reston, and Washington D.C.

Do I need an account to order?

No accounts, no passwords. When you design your bouquet in the studio or pick a signature arrangement, tapping Order opens WhatsApp with your full recipe and price ready to confirm.

Can I include a printed card message?

Yes! You can type up to 140 characters on Step 4 of the studio, and our florists will print it on an artisanal textured card tucked right into the ribbon.

How long will my flowers stay fresh?

Our stems are farm-harvested and hand-conditioned daily. With clean water and our included botanical flower food, our bouquets typically stay vibrant for 7 to 12 days.
