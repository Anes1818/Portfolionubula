Before you start

A few useful answers.

What if a flower is unavailable?+

The design is a request. You confirm availability and agree substitutions with the customer before accepting the order.

Can this use my existing website?+

You can link to your bouquet studio from your website or social profiles. Embedded integrations and payment connections need a separate compatibility review.

Can I add seasonal bouquets?+

Yes, we can review new designs together. We confirm any new artwork, cost and delivery scope before starting.

Does this take customer payments?+

The current builder prepares a design request. You confirm the order and arrange payment through your normal process.

When do I pay?+

There is no payment to explore or send this enquiry. We confirm the scope, you approve your shop’s version, and billing begins after launch. The monthly plan can be cancelled.
